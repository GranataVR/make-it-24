How to play

Combine two numbers with + − × ÷ and the result takes their place. Keep going until one number is left. Make it 24 and the card is yours.

There are 1,362 cards using numbers 1 to 13, and every one has a solution. The dots in the corner show difficulty, like the original 24 cards. Hints show the next move, but peeking at the answer resets your streak.

With friends: one person creates a room and shares the code. Everyone races on the same card with a 5 minute limit, and the host can deal the next one once everybody has solved it or time runs out. Each player gets 3 hints for the whole game, and the answer only shows once you solve the card or time runs out. Share the invite link and friends can join straight from their phone, no account needed.

Keyboard: 1–4 numbers, + - * / operators, Backspace undo, H hint, Enter next card.
